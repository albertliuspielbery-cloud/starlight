This is an offline educational game for children aged 4–6 (Traditional Chinese).
- No account, no ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no data collection (built with the open-source Capacitor framework). All progress is stored on the device.
- Parental gate: "家長設定" (Parent settings) and anything that leaves the app (the support email link, and saving a drawing to Photos via the system share sheet) require answering a multiplication question.
- Microphone: used only in the "跟著唸" (read-aloud) activity so the child can hear their own voice. Recordings stay on the device.
- Chapters 1–5 are free. Chapters 6–20 are unlocked with non-consumable in-app purchases (4 products: chapters 6–10, 11–15, 16–20, or all chapters 6–20; Family Sharing enabled). Stages unlock in order: 11–15 can be bought after 6–10, and 16–20 after 11–15; "all chapters" is offered only before any stage is bought. The purchase screen is ONLY in "家長設定" (Parent Settings), behind the multiplication parental gate: Parent Settings → "解鎖章節". It also has "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) and "輸入兌換碼" (redeem an offer code). When a child taps a locked chapter on the map, the app only says "Ask mom or dad to open new adventures" — no store or price is shown to the child. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit); the app has no server and sends no data.
- App name is 星光小冒險. The title screen shows the story title of the chosen hero: 「星光公主大冒險」 (princess) or 「星光小王子大冒險」 (prince).
- To try the game quickly: choose a hero, enter any name (or skip), then tap "開始冒險".

Kids Category (Guideline 1.3) answers:
1. Third-party analytics: No. 2. Third-party advertising: No.
3. Data shared with third parties: No. The app never connects to any server; all content, fonts and voice audio are bundled. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (StoreKit) only.
4. Other data collection: No. Nickname (optional), progress, settings, drawings and read-aloud recordings are stored only on the device. Recordings are never uploaded and are not in backups.
Nothing leaves the device unless a parent sends it (iOS share sheet / clipboard / own email app), each behind the parental gate. App Privacy: Data Not Collected. Privacy policy: https://albertliuspielbery-cloud.github.io/starlight/privacy.html
