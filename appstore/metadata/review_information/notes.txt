This is an offline educational game for children aged 4–6 (Traditional Chinese).
- No account, no ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no data collection (built with the open-source Capacitor framework). All progress is stored on the device.
- Parental gate: "家長設定" (Parent settings) and anything that leaves the app (the support email link, and saving a drawing to Photos via the system share sheet) require answering a multiplication question.
- Microphone: used only in the "跟著唸" (read-aloud) activity so the child can hear their own voice. Recordings stay on the device.
- Chapters 1–5 are free. NEW in 2.0: the app offers only ONE in-app purchase, the non-consumable tw.starlight.adventure.full ("完整版", Full Version; Family Sharing enabled). It unlocks chapter 6 onward (now chapters 6–45, plus chapters added in later updates), one-time, not a subscription. Its display name was updated to "完整版" in this version. Older products (stage2–stage8, s2to6, s5to8) are no longer sold but stay valid for past buyers (Restore Purchases and offer codes still work). Limited-time gift (since 1.8, ends 2026-12-15 Taiwan time): opening the app unlocks chapters 6–15 for free, permanently (App Store records untouched); the Full Version is still offered. To review: on a fresh install, title screen → "家長專區" (Parents, top-left) → parental gate (e.g. "7 × 5": type 35, tap 確定) → "完整版介紹" (Full version info, in the "說明" group) → tap the big gold button "購買「完整版」" with the price at the TOP of that page (the same purchase row is also at the bottom). If StoreKit cannot load the product, the page shows "重新連接 App Store" (retry) instead. If the sandbox account already owns this product (it has existed since version 1.1), the page says all chapters are unlocked and the parents menu row is named "購買紀錄"; please use a sandbox account that has not bought it. No storefront or device restrictions; the price comes from StoreKit. Chapters 6–45 are then unlocked (played in order). The purchase screen is reachable only after the parental gate ("家長專區" on the title screen or the world map). The page also has "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) and "輸入兌換碼" (redeem an offer code). When a child taps a locked chapter, the app only says "Ask mom or dad to open new adventures" — no store or price is shown to the child.
- App name is 星光小冒險, and the title screen shows the same name (「星光 小冒險」) for both heroes (princess or prince).
- To try the game quickly: choose a hero, enter any name (or skip), then tap "開始冒險".

Kids Category (Guideline 1.3) answers:
1. Third-party analytics: No. 2. Third-party advertising: No.
3. Data shared with third parties: No. The app sends no data to any server; all content, fonts and voice audio are bundled. Its only network request is an anonymous GET of a small public settings file (promo.json, the gift dates) from our GitHub Pages site. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (StoreKit) only.
4. Other data collection: No. Nickname (optional), progress, settings, drawings, read-aloud recordings and 小小攝影師 photos are stored only on the device. Recordings and photos are never uploaded and are not in backups; photos are deleted with the child's profile.
Nothing leaves the device unless a parent sends it (iOS share sheet / clipboard / own email app), each behind the parental gate. App Privacy: Data Not Collected. Privacy policy: https://albertliuspielbery-cloud.github.io/starlight/privacy.html

NEW in 2.0: season 7 (chapters 41–45: subway, library, doctor, zoo, art museum), two playground games and a single Full Version purchase (above). 小小攝影師 does NOT use the device camera: the child frames animals in a drawn zoo scene inside the app and the "photo" is a small picture of that scene, kept only on the device (no camera or photo-library access; no new permissions). 地球儀 is an offline drawn globe. Also a pronunciation fix and small fixes. No data leaves the device.
