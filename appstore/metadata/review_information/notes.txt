This is an offline educational game for children aged 4–6 (Traditional Chinese).
- No account, no ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no data collection (built with the open-source Capacitor framework). All progress is stored on the device.
- Parental gate: "家長設定" (Parent settings) and anything that leaves the app (the support email link, and saving a drawing to Photos via the system share sheet) require answering a multiplication question.
- Microphone: used only in the "跟著唸" (read-aloud) activity so the child can hear their own voice. Recordings stay on the device.
- Chapters 1–5 are free. Chapters 6–35 are unlocked with non-consumable in-app purchases (8 products, Family Sharing enabled): chapters 6–10, 11–15, 16–20, 21–25, 26–30, 31–35, a bundle of chapters 6–20, and a bundle of chapters 6–30. NEW in 1.8: chapters 31–35 (tw.starlight.adventure.stage7, "第三十一～三十五章"). Stages unlock in order (each after the previous one). Both bundles are offered only before any stage is bought; neither bundle includes chapters 31–35 (the 6–20 bundle does not include 21–35, the 6–30 bundle does not include 31–35), as their names say. Limited-time gift in 1.8: for 30 days from 2026-11-15 (device date), opening the app unlocks chapters 6–15 for free, permanently; the app shows a one-time notice. During that period the 第六～十章 and 第十一～十五章 rows in the purchase screen show as already open and cannot be bought (the two bundles stay listed and purchasable); this does not touch App Store purchase records. To review the new product quickly: on a fresh install buy the 6–30 bundle (第六～三十章合購) first; 第三十一～三十五章 then becomes purchasable. The purchase screen is reachable ONLY after the multiplication parental gate, from two parent-labelled entry points: the "家長專區" (Parents) button at the top-left of the title screen → "完整版介紹" (Full version info, in the "說明" group; the purchase rows are at the bottom of that page); and the "家長專區" button on the world map. Both show the parental gate first. The title screen itself has no purchase button. (The gate shows e.g. "7 × 5": type the answer 35 and tap 確定.) Product names in the store: 第六～十章, 第十一～十五章, 第十六～二十章, 第二十一～二十五章, 第二十六～三十章, 第三十一～三十五章, 第六～二十章合購, 第六～三十章合購. It also has "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) and "輸入兌換碼" (redeem an offer code). When a child taps a locked chapter on the map, the app only says "Ask mom or dad to open new adventures" — no store or price is shown to the child. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit); the app has no server and sends no data.
- App name is 星光小冒險, and the title screen shows the same name (「星光 小冒險」) for both heroes (princess or prince).
- To try the game quickly: choose a hero, enter any name (or skip), then tap "開始冒險".

Kids Category (Guideline 1.3) answers:
1. Third-party analytics: No. 2. Third-party advertising: No.
3. Data shared with third parties: No. The app never connects to any server; all content, fonts and voice audio are bundled. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (StoreKit) only.
4. Other data collection: No. Nickname (optional), progress, settings, drawings and read-aloud recordings are stored only on the device. Recordings are never uploaded and are not in backups.
Nothing leaves the device unless a parent sends it (iOS share sheet / clipboard / own email app), each behind the parental gate. App Privacy: Data Not Collected. Privacy policy: https://albertliuspielbery-cloud.github.io/starlight/privacy.html
